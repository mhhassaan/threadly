# Threadly logo concept v1

## Concept
Three horizontal reading lines are joined into one continuous thread. The mark symbolizes a long article becoming a connected, digestible reading sequence. A compact blue symbol is paired with a lowercase wordmark.

## Final deliverable
- threadly-logo-v1.png: selected horizontal logo on a white background.
- Format: generated raster PNG; this is a design concept, not a vector master.
- Intended palette: blue #1D9BF0, near-black #0F1419, white #FFFFFF.
- Created using the built-in ImageGen tool. No app source files were changed and the logo has not been installed in the navbar.

## Generation prompts

### Initial concept
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: Primary horizontal logo for Threadly, a reading app that turns long articles into coherent, detailed Twitter-style threads.
Create a finished, premium, modern, sleek and minimal brand identity: an original compact symbol at the left and the exact lowercase wordmark "threadly" at the right. The symbol should elegantly fuse the idea of a page of text being distilled into a connected sequence of conversation: three simple rounded horizontal strokes or compact conversation forms connected by a single clever thread, subtly suggesting a lowercase t through negative space. Make it a distinctive balanced logo silhouette, not a literal flowchart or a generic bulleted list. Very few elements, generous negative space, consistent optical weight, recognizable at 24px. Symbol in crisp blue #1D9BF0; wordmark in deep near-black #0F1419, with refined contemporary grotesk lettering, confident simple shapes, careful kerning. No tagline. Flat, vector-like precision, solid fills, no gradients, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, no bird, no X, no hashtag, no book illustration, no extra text, no mockup or brand presentation sheet. One primary logo only, centered on a genuinely transparent background, spacious landscape composition. The blue symbol and lowercase wordmark must form one beautifully proportioned professional app logo. Do not draw a background or a checkerboard.

### Geometric refinement
Refine this Threadly logo into an exceptionally clean, flat, production-ready brand mark. Preserve the exact lowercase "threadly" wordmark and the horizontal icon-plus-wordmark layout. Refine only the blue icon and edge quality: it should be a crisp, controlled geometric continuous thread connecting three horizontal text strokes, with the upper stroke longest and each subsequent stroke a little shorter. Use a consistent, slimmer monoline weight, generous clean negative-space gaps, precise rounded terminals, and balanced optical spacing. It should read as a long article distilled into a connected thread, not a puffy ribbon. All blue areas must be perfectly uniform solid #1D9BF0, with clean smooth edges and no texture, mottling, bevels, gradients, or artifacts. Wordmark is solid #0F1419. Keep it sophisticated and minimal, legible as a small navigation logo. Transparent background, no extra text, no watermark, no mockup. One final horizontal logo only.

### Final selected artwork cleanup
Use case: logo-brand, precise cleanup of this primary Threadly logo. Keep the exact horizontal arrangement, lowercase wordmark "threadly", and the connected three-stroke symbol. Redraw the entire logo from clean geometric shapes on a perfectly solid pure white #FFFFFF background. The symbol must be a single perfectly uniform solid blue #1D9BF0 shape with smooth edges. All interior gaps must be pristine pure white, completely empty of blue, cyan, noise, speckles, remnants or texture. The wordmark must be perfectly uniform near-black #0F1419 with smooth clean edges. Preserve a minimal, professional, highly legible identity. Absolutely no gradients, shadows, mottling, distressed edges, glow, lighting, 3D or mockup. Output a polished finished horizontal logo on white, centered with balanced padding. This is the final logo artwork, not a presentation sheet.

The exploratory transparent versions and icon exploration were not selected as final assets.
